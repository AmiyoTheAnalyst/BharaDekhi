Frequently Asked Questions

Learn how BharaDekhi estimates fares and helps plan trips around Dhaka.

How are the fare estimates calculated?

Ride fare estimates use the selected route’s OSRM road distance and duration with baseline rates for bike, CNG, and car. Dhaka-time peak windows are estimated as 8:30–10:30 AM and 5:00–8:00 PM; fares use an illustrative 1.4x–2.2x peak range. Current rain for central Dhaka is detected using Open-Meteo weather data, checked every 15 minutes. When rain is detected, an illustrative 1.3x–1.5x uplift applies to ride and feeder estimates. Use the rain fare selector to force the uplift on or off. If weather data is unavailable, automatic mode shows a status and does not add the uplift unless a prior reading is available. Metro and local bus fares are not weather-adjusted. Vehicle comparison times use estimated average road speeds (15 km/h at peak and 22 km/h off-peak), adjusted by mode. The MRT Line 6 planner compares rickshaw, bike, and CNG estimates for each station-access leg, highlights the cheapest, and shows where to board and get off. The custom rickshaw feeder estimate is ৳20 + ৳12/km (rounded up to ৳10) for legs up to 5 km; longer legs are marked unavailable. The local bus finder shows direct matches and up to three bus + feeder suggestions by connecting nearby route stops to each endpoint. It displays the feeder modes, boarding and exit stops, estimated total fare, and approximate journey time. Bus and feeder routes, distances, fares, and journey times are draft estimates, not verified BRTA data; long feeder legs may not be practical. Confirm availability and fare locally before travel.

How do peak hours affect rickshaw feeder fares?

Rickshaw estimates allow for bargaining: the peak-hour lower estimate stays the same, and only the upper estimate increases by ৳10. For example, a ৳40–৳50 non-peak estimate becomes ৳40–৳60 at peak. This applies to rickshaw feeder legs; bike, CNG, direct ride, and rain estimates follow their own adjustments.

Are these the official Uber or Pathao prices?

No. These are independent estimates, not live provider quotes. Each ride service sets its own fare, availability, fees, and surge pricing. Check the provider’s app for the final price before booking.

When does BharaDekhi consider it peak hour in Dhaka?

The current estimate uses Dhaka local time and treats 8:30–10:30 AM and 5:00–8:00 PM as approximate peak windows. Peak fare factors are illustrative (1.4x–2.2x), not live provider prices. Actual traffic and provider surge pricing can differ.

Do I need an account to use the route planner?

Yes. Sign in or create an account to open the route planner, compare ride options, and use the links to ride services.
