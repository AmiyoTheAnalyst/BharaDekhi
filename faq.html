Frequently Asked Questions

Learn how BharaDekhi estimates fares and helps plan trips around Dhaka.

How are the fare estimates calculated?

BharaDekhi estimates fares from the selected route’s road distance and estimated travel time, using baseline rates for bike, CNG, and car. The estimates are for comparison and may not match a provider’s final price.

Are these the official Uber, Pathao, or inDrive prices?

No. These are independent estimates, not live provider quotes. Each ride service sets its own fare, availability, fees, and surge pricing. Check the provider’s app for the final price before booking.

When does BharaDekhi consider it peak hour in Dhaka?

The current estimate uses Dhaka local time and treats 8:30–10:30 AM and 5:30–8:30 PM as approximate weekday-style peak windows. Actual traffic and provider surge pricing can differ.

Do I need an account to use the route planner?

Yes. Sign in or create an account to open the route planner, compare ride options, and use the links to ride services.
