Featured Transit Platform & City Simulation

BharaDekhi — Multi-Modal Transit Engine & Living Dhaka Crossroads Simulator

Live Site GitHub

A production-grade Dhaka multi-modal transit planning and fare estimation web platform. Features an authentic 2.5D Living Dhaka Crossroads landing page (Mirpur 10 junction with 4-phase coordinated traffic signals, moving multi-modal vehicles, zebra-crossing pedestrians, and illuminated Dhaka skyline), two-screen SPA hash navigation, interactive 'More Ways to Travel' public transport comparison (Metro Rail vs. Local Bus) with lowest total route fare calculations, multi-modal feeder options (Rickshaw, CNG, Bike, Car), live OSRM road distance routing, Leaflet.js geocoding, automated trip playback animations, Open-Meteo rain detection, peak surge multiplier tachometers, MRT Line 6 viaduct light-path tracking, local bus finder, particle burst launch links for Uber and Pathao, and zero-layout-shift English/Bangla localization.
