More connected BD ideas.

Building on the connected-letter direction, here are a few more ways to combine BharaDekhi initials, route lines, and pickup-to-destination markers.

01

Route Pin

A familiar map pin with a road running through it: direct, friendly, and easy to recognize.

Meaning: pickup · destination · journeyStrongest map connection
02

Connected BD

A compact monogram built around two stops and one continuous road. More ownable as an app badge.

Meaning: BharaDekhi initials · connected stopsMost distinctive silhouette
03

Dhaka Wayfinder

A clean compass and route motif with a green accent. Calm, modern, and made for a helpful local guide.

Meaning: guidance · direction · city routesMost polished and versatile
04

One Continuous Route

The B flows into the D as one uninterrupted road, with two stops marking the start and finish.

Meaning: the initials become the route itselfBlack background · cyan route · gold endpoints
05

Two Stops, One BD

A bright, open monogram with an obvious origin and destination—made to stay legible at small sizes.

Meaning: easy start and end pointsBlack background · mint route · gold start
06

Street Grid Monogram

A compact BD built from intersecting streets, with a small green destination point for a subtle Dhaka map feel.

Meaning: local streets stitched togetherMost urban and map-like

Concept 05 is now used on the BharaDekhi site with the “Route Planner & Fare Estimator” tagline. Other concepts remain visual explorations only.
